Privacy Policy – ZarnisCaisse

Last updated: 2026

ZarnisCaisse respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when using the ZarnisCaisse mobile application.

Information We Collect

The application may collect or process the following information in order to provide its services.

Camera Access

The application may access the device camera only to:

  • Scan barcodes
  • Scan receipts or products

Camera data is not stored or transmitted outside the device.

Account Information

When users create an account or sign in, the application may collect:

  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials

This information is used only to identify the user and allow secure access to the application.

Business Data

The application allows users to manage business-related data such as:

  • Products
  • Inventory
  • Suppliers
  • Transactions
  • Sales reports

This information is stored locally on the device and may optionally be synchronized with cloud services.

How We Use Information

The collected information is used only to operate and improve the application, including:

  • Managing inventory and products
  • Recording sales transactions
  • Generating financial reports
  • Synchronizing data between devices
  • Authenticating users securely

ZarnisCaisse does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Data Storage

By default, most data is stored locally on the user's device. Optional cloud synchronization may store data securely online when users activate cloud features or multi-user functionality.

Data Security

ZarnisCaisse implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. All data transmitted over the internet is encrypted during transfer using secure protocols (HTTPS / TLS).

Third-Party Services

ZarnisCaisse may use trusted third-party services to operate certain features, such as:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Services

These services may process limited user data necessary for authentication and secure cloud synchronization.

Account Deletion

Users have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated data. To request account deletion, users can send an email to zarniscaisse.support@gmail.com. The request should include:

  • The email address associated with the account
  • The organization or business name (if applicable)

After verification, the account and associated data will be permanently deleted from our systems. The deletion process may take up to 7 days. Certain information may be temporarily retained if required for legal obligations or security purposes.

Deleting Business Data

Users can delete the following data directly from the application:

  • Products
  • Suppliers
  • Inventory records

To delete this data: open the Stock Management section of the application, select the item you want to delete, then tap the Delete option.

Transaction data

Transactions are archived automatically and may be permanently deleted after a defined retention period.

User Rights

Users may:

  • Request deletion of their account
  • Request information about stored data
  • Stop using cloud synchronization at any time

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the application or legal requirements. Users are encouraged to review this page regularly.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data deletion requests, please contact: zarniscaisse.support@gmail.com
